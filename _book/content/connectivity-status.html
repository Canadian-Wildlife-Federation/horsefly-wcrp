Current Connectivity Status

In the Stewiacke watershed, 1005.73 km of Atlantic Salmon habitat are currently connected to the ocean and 1414.91 km are disconnected from it. This means that 41.5% of the 2,420.64 km of total habitat is connected.

In the Stewiacke watershed, 891.94 km of American Eel habitat are currently connected to the ocean and 981.76 km are disconnected from it. This means that 47.6% of the 1,873.7 km of total habitat is connected.

Structure Count

In the Stewiacke watershed, 3403 structures potentially disconnect Atlantic Salmon habitat (Figure 1), and 1078 structures potentially disconnect American Eel habitat (Figure 2). Based on previous assessments, 15 of these structures require additional fish-passage assessments, and 4 require in-depth habitat assessments.
